### What I understood
You purchased a phone for Rs 18,000 on September 2. You returned it on September 5 because it was defective. The company collected the item, but you have not received your refund after two months, and customer support is not resolving the issue.

### Where you stand
Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, refusing to refund money for defective goods or services is considered an unfair trade practice [1]. If a seller refuses to refund the amount for defective goods, you have the right to file a formal complaint with the District Commission [2].

### What you can do
1.  **Final Written Notice:** Send a formal email or letter to the company’s grievance officer (check their website for this contact). Clearly state the order ID, date of return, proof of pickup, and the amount due. Give them a final deadline (e.g., 7 days) to process the refund before you escalate the matter.
2.  **National Consumer Helpline:** If they do not respond, call the National Consumer Helpline at **1915** or register your grievance on the [NCH website](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/).
3.  **e-Daakhil:** If the issue remains unresolved, you can file a formal complaint electronically through the [e-Daakhil portal](https://edaakhil.nic.in/). This allows you to file a complaint with the District Commission without needing to visit the court physically [2].

### Proof to collect
*   **Order Confirmation:** The original invoice or order receipt showing the date and amount (Rs 18,000).
*   **Return Proof:** The return request confirmation, the courier pickup receipt, or any email/SMS confirming the item was picked up.
*   **Communication History:** Screenshots of chats, emails, or call logs with customer care where they acknowledged the return or promised a refund.

### When to get a lawyer
You generally do not need a lawyer to file a complaint in the District Consumer Commission, as the process is designed for consumers to represent themselves. However, you should consult a lawyer if:
*   The company sends you a legal notice.
*   The case becomes complex or the company contests the claim in court, and you feel uncomfortable handling the legal procedures yourself.
