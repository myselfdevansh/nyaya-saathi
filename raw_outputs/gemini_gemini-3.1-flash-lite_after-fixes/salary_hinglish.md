**1. What I understood:**
Aapne bataya ki aapki company ne pichle 3 mahine se salary nahi di hai aur ab unhone office bhi band kar diya hai. Aap Pune mein kaam karte the.

**2. Where you stand:**
Kanoon ke hisaab se, agar aapka employer aapko salary nahi deta hai, toh yeh ek serious matter hai. *Code on Wages, 2019, Section 59* ke mutabik, agar aap salary na milne ki shikayat karte hain, toh yeh saabit karne ki zimmedari employer ki hoti hai ki unhone aapko payment kar diya hai. Yani, agar aapne claim file kiya, toh employer ko proof dena padega ki unhone paise diye hain.

**3. What you can do:**
Aap ye steps follow kar sakte hain:
*   **Written Complaint:** Sabse pehle apne employer ko ek formal email ya registered post bhejiye jisme saaf likha ho ki aapki kitni salary pending hai aur aapne kab tak kaam kiya tha. Iska record sambhaal kar rakhein.
*   **Labour Commissioner Office:** Pune mein Labour Commissioner ka office hota hai. Aap wahan jaakar apni shikayat darj kar sakte hain. Woh aapke employer ko notice bhej sakte hain.
*   **Legal Notice:** Agar company respond nahi karti, toh aap ek lawyer ke zariye company ko "Legal Notice" bhej sakte hain.
*   **Free Legal Aid:** Agar aapko financial dikkat hai, toh aap NALSA ki helpline **15100** par call karke free legal advice ya madad maang sakte hain.

**4. Proof to collect:**
Apne case ko mazboot karne ke liye ye documents ikattha karein:
*   Appointment letter ya offer letter.
*   Salary slips (agar hain toh).
*   Bank statement jisme dikhe ki pichle mahino mein salary aayi thi aur ab nahi aa rahi.
*   Company ke saath hui koi bhi email ya WhatsApp chat jisme salary ki baat hui ho.
*   Office band hone ka koi proof (jaise koi notice ya photo).

**5. When to get a lawyer:**
Agar Labour Commissioner ke office se koi hal nahi nikalta, ya company ka pata nahi chal raha hai aur amount bada hai, toh aapko ek labour law expert lawyer ki zaroorat padegi jo aapke liye court mein case file kar sake.

**Kuch sawal:**
1. Kya aapke paas koi written appointment letter ya contract hai?
2. Kya aapke paas company ke kisi senior ya HR ka contact number ya email hai?
3. Kya aapke saath aur bhi employees hain jinki salary nahi mili?
