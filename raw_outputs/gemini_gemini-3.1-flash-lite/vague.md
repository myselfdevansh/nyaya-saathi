### Where you stand
Under the law, "cheating" is a serious offense. If someone has dishonestly induced you to deliver property, money, or valuable security, or has deceived you, they may be liable for punishment under the law.

Specifically, the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)** addresses cheating in several ways:
*   **Section 318:** Defines cheating generally. If someone cheats you and induces you to deliver property or valuable security, they can face imprisonment up to seven years and a fine [1].
*   **Section 319:** Covers "cheating by personation," where someone pretends to be someone else to cheat you [2].
*   **Section 322:** Covers situations where someone executes a deed or document containing false statements about the consideration (money/value) involved [3].
