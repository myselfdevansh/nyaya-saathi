### 1. What I understood
You bought a phone online for Rs 18,000 on 2 September. Because it was defective, you initiated a return on 5 September. The item was picked up, but even after 2 months, you have not received your refund, and customer support keeps giving vague responses.

---

### 2. Where you stand
* Under consumer protection law, refusing or failing to refund the money paid for a returned defective product—either within the stipulated period or within 30 days—is an unfair trade practice [1].
* A seller who fails to refund your money or deal with a defective product can be held liable [2].
* If you approach a Consumer Forum (District Commission), it has the power to order the seller/platform to refund your Rs 18,000 along with interest, and even pay you compensation for mental harassment or financial loss [3].

---

### 3. What you can do
1. **Send a final written Complaint / Legal Notice:** Send a formal email or registered letter to the seller and the e-commerce portal giving them a strict deadline (e.g., 7 days) to issue the full refund of Rs 18,000 plus interest, failing which you will file a consumer case.
2. **Call National Consumer Helpline (NCH):** Call **1915** or register a complaint online on the National Consumer Helpline portal (consumerhelpline.gov.in) or via their app. Many major online platforms resolve issues quickly once an NCH ticket is raised.
3. **File a case on e-Daakhil:** If they still do not refund your money, you can file an official consumer complaint online via the **e-Daakhil** portal (edaakhil.nic.in) to your local District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission to claim your refund and compensation [3].

---

### 4. Proof to collect
Gather and save digital/physical copies of the following:
* **Invoice/Bill:** Showing the purchase amount (Rs 18,000) and order date (2 September).
* **Return & Pickup Receipt:** Proof of return request (5 September), return tracking status, and pickup confirmation receipt/SMS.
* **Communication Records:** Screenshots of all emails, chats, and records of customer support calls where they promised the refund was "in process".
* **Bank Statement:** Showing the deduction of Rs 18,000 and the absence of any credited refund to date.

---

### 5. When to get a lawyer
For a claim of Rs 18,000, you can easily file a complaint yourself on the e-Daakhil portal without a lawyer. However, consider consulting a lawyer if:
* The e-commerce company completely denies receiving the returned item.
* You need help drafting a formal legal notice.
* You are uncomfortable presenting the case yourself before the District Commission. 

You can also contact the **NALSA Legal Aid Helpline at 15100** for free advice if needed.
