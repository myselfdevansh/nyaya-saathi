### 1. What I understood
Your friend gave you a cheque of ₹50,000 to repay a loan, but the cheque was dishonoured (bounced) when presented to the bank.

---

### 2. Where you stand
* **Dishonour of Cheque:** A cheque is dishonoured by non-payment when default in payment is made upon presentation [1].
* **Drawer’s Liability:** The person who wrote the cheque (the drawer) is bound to compensate you if the cheque bounces, provided proper notice of dishonour is given [2].
* **Cheque Bounce Law:** Under the law, if a cheque issued to clear a legally enforceable debt or liability bounces due to insufficient funds (or similar bank reasons), it is a punishable offence. However, you must first issue a formal written demand notice to the drawer giving them 15 days to pay the amount [3].
* **Interim Relief:** During a court trial under Section 138, the court may direct the drawer to pay you an interim compensation of up to 20% of the cheque amount [4].

---

### 3. What you can do (Step-by-Step)
1. **Send a Legal Demand Notice:** Within 30 days of receiving the cheque bounce memo from your bank, send a formal written notice to your friend demanding payment of ₹50,000 within 15 days of receiving the notice.
2. **Wait for 15 Days:** Give your friend 15 days from the date they receive the notice to make the payment [3].
3. **File a Criminal Complaint:** If your friend fails to pay within those 15 days, you can file a formal complaint under Section 138 before the magistrate court within 1 month from the end of the 15-day period.

---

### 4. Proof to collect
Gather and keep the following documents safe:
* The original bounced cheque.
* The original Bank Return Memo (cheque return memo stating the reason for dishonour).
* Proof of loan/debt (whatsapp messages, bank transfer proof, agreement, or receipt showing you lent ₹50,000).
* Copy of the Legal Demand Notice sent.
* Postal receipt / Speed Post tracking report confirming delivery of the notice.

---

### 5. When to get a lawyer
You should contact an advocate immediately to draft and send the formal legal demand notice within the mandatory 30-day timeline. A lawyer will ensure the statutory timelines are strictly met so your legal remedy remains valid.
