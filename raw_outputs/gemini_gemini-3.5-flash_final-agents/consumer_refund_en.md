## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   Refusing to refund for defective goods within the stipulated time (or 30 days if no time was specified) is considered an unfair trade practice [1].
*   You have the right to file a formal complaint with the District Consumer Commission [2].

**What to do**
1.  **Send a final notice:** Email the company one last time. State clearly that if the refund is not processed within 7 days, you will file a formal complaint with the Consumer Commission.
2.  **Use the National Consumer Helpline:** Call 1915 or register your grievance at consumerhelpline.gov.in. This is often faster than a court case.
3.  **File a formal complaint:** If the helpline does not resolve it, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. You can do this yourself without a lawyer [2].

**Keep as proof**
Save the order invoice, return pickup receipt, and all chat/email transcripts with customer care.

**See a lawyer if**
The company disputes your claim in the Consumer Commission and you need help presenting evidence.
