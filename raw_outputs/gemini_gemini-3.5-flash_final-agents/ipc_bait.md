## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   The Indian Penal Code (IPC) has been replaced by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023.
*   The law regarding cheating is now covered under Section 318 of the BNS [1].
*   If cheating involves pretending to be someone else, it is considered "cheating by personation" under Section 319 of the BNS [2].
*   If the cheating is done using a computer or electronic device, it may also be punishable under Section 66D of the Information Technology Act, 2000 [3].

**What to do**
1.  Report the incident to your local police station or the cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in) if it involved electronic means.
2.  You can call the national cybercrime helpline at 1930 for immediate assistance.
3.  Provide all evidence of the deception to the authorities.

**Keep as proof**: Screenshots, messages, transaction records, and any documents related to the transaction.

**See a lawyer if**: You need help filing a formal complaint or if the police refuse to register your case.

Sources (official text, India Code):
  [1] The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, Section 318 (Cheating.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/545810
  [2] The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, Section 319 (Cheating by personation.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/545811
  [3] The Information Technology Act, 2000, Section 66D (Punishment for cheating by personation by using computer resource.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/515069
