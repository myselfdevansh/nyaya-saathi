## Assistant
**Where you stand**:
*   Aapki company ne 3 mahine ki salary nahi di hai aur ab office bhi band kar diya hai.
*   Employer par proof karne ki zimmedari hai ki usne aapko payment kar diya hai [1].
*   Aap "worker" ki definition mein aate hain, agar aap managerial/administrative capacity mein nahi the ya supervisory capacity mein ₹18,000 se zyada wages nahi le rahe the [2].

**What to do**:
1.  Apne employer ko ek formal written notice bhejein (email ya registered post se) jismein unpaid salary ka amount aur period mention ho, aur payment ki demand karein.
2.  Apne local Labour Commissioner office mein complaint file karein. Yeh unpaid wages recover karne ka ek tareeka ho sakta hai.
3.  Samadhan portal (samadhan.labour.gov.in) par online complaint file karne ki koshish karein.
4.  Agar aapka employer koi social security contribution (jaise PF) bhi nahi de raha tha, toh uske liye bhi aap complaint kar sakte hain [3].

**Keep as proof**: Employment contract, offer letter, payslips (agar koi hain), bank statements, attendance records, aur aapki bheji hui notice ki copy.

**See a lawyer if**: Aapko Labour Commissioner se madad nahi milti ya aapko legal process mein guidance chahiye.
