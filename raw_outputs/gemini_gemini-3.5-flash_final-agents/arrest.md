## Assistant
⚠ This involves police or court proceedings with deadlines. Please speak to a lawyer today. Free legal aid: call NALSA 15100 or visit your District Legal Services Authority.
**Where you stand**
*   The police must immediately inform the arrested person of the full details of the offence or grounds for the arrest [1].
*   The police must inform a relative or friend nominated by the arrested person about the arrest and the place of detention [2].
*   The arrested person has the right to meet an advocate of their choice during interrogation [3].
*   If the arrest was for a bailable offence, the police must inform the person of their right to be released on bail [1].

**What to do**
1.  Go to the police station immediately and ask for the "arrest memo" to confirm the grounds of arrest and witness details [4].
2.  Demand to know if your brother has nominated you or someone else to be informed of his arrest [2].
3.  Request to speak with your brother to confirm he is safe and has been informed of his rights.

**Keep as proof**: Any written communication or acknowledgment from the police station.

**See a lawyer if**: The police refuse to provide the grounds for arrest or deny you access to your brother.
