**What to do**:
1. Log in to the official Income Tax e-Filing portal (incometax.gov.in) to view the specific notice and the reason for it.
2. Carefully read the notice to understand if it is for a mismatch in TDS, non-filing of returns, or a demand for tax.
3. Respond directly through the e-Filing portal by selecting the "e-Proceeding" tab.
4. If you are unsure how to respond, do not ignore the notice, as it may lead to penalties.

**See a lawyer if**:
You receive a notice for a large amount, a penalty, or if you do not understand the legal basis of the demand; consult a Chartered Accountant (CA) or a tax lawyer.
