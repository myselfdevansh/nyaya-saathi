## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   As a senior citizen unable to maintain yourself from your own earnings or property, you are entitled to claim maintenance from your children [1].
*   If your son neglects or refuses to provide for your needs, a Maintenance Tribunal can order him to pay a monthly maintenance allowance [2].
*   If you transferred property to your son on the condition that he would provide for your basic needs and he has failed to do so, you can ask the Tribunal to declare that transfer void [3].

**What to do**
1.  File an application for maintenance before the Maintenance Tribunal in your district [4].
2.  You can seek assistance from local organizations or legal aid services if you are unable to file the application yourself [3].
3.  If the Tribunal passes an order in your favor, keep a copy of it, as it is enforceable by law [5].

**Keep as proof**
Any documents showing the transfer of your savings or property to your son, and any evidence of his refusal to provide for your needs.

**See a lawyer if**
You need help drafting the application or if your son refuses to comply with the Tribunal's order. You can also call the NALSA helpline at 15100 for free legal aid.
