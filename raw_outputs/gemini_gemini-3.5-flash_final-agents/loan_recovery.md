## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   Banks and financial institutions are expected to follow fair practices. While they can recover debts, harassment or abuse is not permitted.
*   If you are facing issues with a bank, you must first complain to the bank directly. If they do not resolve it, you can escalate the matter to the RBI Ombudsman.

**What to do**
1.  **Complain to the bank:** Write a formal email or letter to the bank's grievance officer immediately. State clearly that recovery agents are harassing you and your family. Keep a copy of this complaint.
2.  **Escalate to RBI:** If the bank does not resolve the issue or does not respond, file a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in) or call their contact centre at 14448.
3.  **Report harassment:** If recovery agents threaten you or use abusive language, report this to the local police station or call 112.
4.  **Document calls:** Keep a log of the dates, times, and phone numbers of the harassing calls.

**Keep as proof**
Copies of your written complaint to the bank and the reference number provided by them.

**See a lawyer if**
You receive a formal legal notice regarding property seizure under the SARFAESI Act [1].
