**What to do**
1.  Check your signed rental agreement for specific clauses regarding the timeline and conditions for the return of your security deposit.
2.  Send a formal written notice (via email or registered post) to your landlord demanding the return of the deposit, referencing the agreement terms.
3.  If the landlord refuses, consult a local lawyer to send a legal notice, as this is a civil dispute.
4.  You may approach the local civil court for recovery of the amount if the landlord continues to withhold it.

**Keep as proof**
Your rental agreement, proof of payment of the deposit, and all communication (emails/messages) regarding vacating the property.

**See a lawyer if**
The landlord refuses to return the deposit despite a formal request, as you will need legal assistance to initiate recovery proceedings.
