## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   If the builder fails to give possession by the date specified in your agreement, you have the right to withdraw from the project and demand a full refund of your money with interest [1].
*   Alternatively, if you choose to stay in the project, you are entitled to interest for every month of delay until possession is handed over [1].
*   You are also entitled to claim compensation for any loss caused by the delay [2].

**What to do**
1.  Check your "Agreement for Sale" to confirm the promised possession date and the interest rate mentioned for delays.
2.  File a formal complaint with your state's RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) online portal to claim your refund or interest.
3.  If the builder refuses to comply with RERA orders, they may face penalties [3].

**Keep as proof**: Your payment receipts, the signed Agreement for Sale, and all communication with the builder.

**See a lawyer if**: The builder refuses to refund you even after a RERA order or if the project is not registered under RERA.

  [3] The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, Section 67 (Penalty for failure to comply with orders of Authority by allottee.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/545195
