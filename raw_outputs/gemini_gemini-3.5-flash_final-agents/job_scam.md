**Where you stand**
*   This is a case of cheating. If the person dishonestly induced you to part with money by promising a job they had no intention or ability to provide, it is a criminal offence [citation removed: unverified].
*   If the person forged documents or falsified records to trick you, they may also be liable for forgery or falsification of accounts [1], [2].

**What to do**
1.  **Report immediately:** If the transaction was online, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. If offline, go to your nearest police station to file an FIR.
2.  **Contact your bank:** If you paid via bank transfer or UPI, immediately inform your bank to freeze the transaction or report the fraud.
3.  **File a complaint:** Provide the police with all details of the person, including their phone number, bank account details, and any communication (chats/emails).

**Keep as proof**
Save all screenshots of chats, call logs, bank transaction receipts, and any documents provided by the person.

**See a lawyer if**
The police refuse to register your FIR or if you need help drafting a formal complaint.
