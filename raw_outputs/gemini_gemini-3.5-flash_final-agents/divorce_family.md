## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   Divorce laws in India depend on your religion. The process and grounds differ significantly between personal laws.
*   If you are Christian, you may file for divorce on specific grounds like adultery, cruelty, or desertion [1].
*   If you and your spouse have lived separately for two years and mutually agree to dissolve the marriage, you may file for divorce by mutual consent [2].

**What to do**
1.  Identify your personal law (e.g., Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Special Marriage Act) as this determines which court and rules apply.
2.  Consult a lawyer to draft a petition based on your specific grounds (e.g., cruelty, desertion, or mutual consent).
3.  File the petition in the District Court having jurisdiction over your area [1].
4.  If you are in immediate danger, call 112 or the women's helpline 181.

**Keep as proof**: Marriage certificate, photos, and any evidence of grounds for divorce (e.g., proof of separation).

**See a lawyer if**: You are filing for a contested divorce, need custody of children, or require financial maintenance. For free legal aid, call NALSA at 15100.

Sources (official text, India Code):

  [2] The Divorce Act, 1869, Section 10A (Dissolution of marriage by mutual consent.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/536351
