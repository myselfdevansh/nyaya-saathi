## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   If you are a "worker" (not in a managerial/administrative role or a supervisor earning over ₹18,000/month), your employer must follow specific rules for retrenchment [1].
*   Since you have completed more than one year of continuous service, your employer was required to provide one month's written notice or pay wages in lieu of that notice [2].
*   You are also entitled to retrenchment compensation equivalent to 15 days' average pay for every completed year of service [2].

**What to do**
1.  Write a formal email or letter to your HR/Management requesting your notice pay and retrenchment compensation as per the Industrial Relations Code.
2.  If they refuse, contact your local Labour Commissioner’s office to file a complaint regarding illegal retrenchment.
3.  You can also register your grievance on the Samadhan portal (samadhan.labour.gov.in).

**Keep as proof**: Your appointment letter, salary slips, and any communication regarding your termination.

**See a lawyer if**: The company refuses to pay your dues or if you believe your termination was due to discrimination or unfair labour practices.

Sources (official text, India Code):
  [1] The Industrial Relations Code, 2020, Section 2 (Definitions.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/512178
  [2] The Industrial Relations Code, 2020, Section 70 (Conditions precedent to retrenchment of workers.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/512242
