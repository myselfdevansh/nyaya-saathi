## Assistant
**Where you stand**
*   An insurer must provide written communication detailing the specific grounds and materials used to reject your claim [1].
*   The insurer must prove that the suppressed fact was material and had a direct bearing on the risk; if they cannot prove you knew or that it was material, the rejection may be invalid [1].
*   If the policy was issued more than two years ago, the insurer faces stricter limits on questioning the policy based on misstatements [1].

**What to do**
1.  **Demand written reasons:** If you haven't received a formal rejection letter, write to the insurer immediately demanding the specific grounds and medical evidence they used to reject your claim [1].
2.  **Internal Grievance:** File a formal complaint through the insurer’s internal grievance portal.
3.  **Escalate:** If unresolved, approach the Insurance Ombudsman (cioins.co.in) or Bima Bharosa (bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in).
4.  **Consumer Complaint:** If the issue persists, file a complaint at e-jagriti.gov.in.

**Keep as proof**: All correspondence with the insurer, the rejection letter, and your medical records.

**See a lawyer if**: The claim amount is very high or the insurer refuses to provide the evidence they used to reject your claim.

Sources (official text, India Code):
  [1] The Insurance Act, 1938, Section 45 (Policy not to be called in question on ground of mis-statement after two years.) https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/517771
